# Forge 全局工作台 · Operate

Scope: replace the visual world across the existing Forge shell and generated workbench while preserving every route, state transition, field, permission, validation rule and business action.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A luminous technical-paper workbench makes source, state and next action legible without card noise; it refuses both generic rounded dashboards and dense legacy admin chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white and graphite planes, hairline neutral separators, one Forge ember accent, concentric 6/8/12/16px radii, flat authored surfaces, tabular technical details and restrained ambient-light overlays.

STORY: Users orient in the shell, scan operational truth, enter a focused production/review/delivery task, inspect exact evidence, act with confidence and retain their place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A quiet 208px rail, 48px utility bar, restrained page heading, inline operational summary and continuous task surface; the primary action sits at the heading edge, never inside a decorative hero.

FORM: Sixth grounded direction, “luminous technical paper,” seed a6270571. Cloud-edge restraint contributes achromatic fields and color confined to state edges; all pictorial, angled, dimensional and saturated challenger grammar is declined.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: comfortable density for overview, AI and settings; compact density for tables, files, review, diff-like evidence and logs. Light and dark modes must use the same semantic system. Motion is limited to state, overlay and spatial-continuity feedback.
